## Upgrade from v0.1.0 with a password-protected REST repository (#48)

This is checked by hand, because no e2e covers it. v0.1.0 came from the public installer
(`get.cloudground.it`) on the stock box. It backed up to `rest:https://cgrest:<password>@<host>:8000/cg/`,
a `rest-server` 0.14 with htpasswd authentication and a Let's Encrypt certificate. On v0.1.0 the
password was in clear in `panel_settings.backup_repository` and in every `backups.repository` row.
Then the box was moved to the candidate with `CLOUDGROUND_LOCAL_BUILD`:

- in `state.db`, its WAL and the logical dump, the password occurs 0 times; the columns hold
  `rest:https://cgrest:{sealed}@…`; the API shows it masked;
- a new backup and its restore test passed; the restore test of a backup taken on v0.1.0 passed
  again; a files restore in place brought back the marker file, and the site served it;
- the agent's and the API's journals do not contain the password;
- `cgctl uninstall` wrote the real password back into `backup.env`, ready for restic. Its
  end-of-run summary also printed the password, now masked (#90).

(static alone spreads 9.8 % over the three runs). Uncached is the only change beyond the spread,
+10 %, with its three runs within 1.8 % of each other. That matches the PHP-FPM work of #10

v0.1.0-versus-candidate A/B on one box (`cgbench ab`) would settle it. No scenario had an error
