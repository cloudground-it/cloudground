# Code of conduct

CloudGround's issues, discussions, pull requests and reviews are places to work on software
together. Everyone taking part, maintainers included, agrees to keep them that way.

## Expected

- Talk about the work, not the person. Disagree with an idea by giving reasons.
- Assume the other side means well and may be missing context you have, or the other way round.
- Write for someone reading in their second language: plain words, no sarcasm.
- Accept a "no" on a change with grace, and give one with an explanation.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## Not accepted

- Insults, slurs, mockery, and comments about anyone's identity, body, background or abilities.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private messages, including continued contact after
  being asked to stop.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Repeatedly derailing a discussion, or pressuring people for free work on a deadline.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer privately, **@manuto276** on GitHub, with links to what happened. A
report is read in confidence; the person reporting is not named to the other party without their
agreement. If the maintainer is the one involved, say so in the report and it will be handled by
someone else the project trusts.

## What happens

Depending on what happened: a private word, a public reminder of this page, removal of comments,
a temporary block or, for serious or repeated cases, a permanent one. The decision and its reason
are told to the person concerned.

This applies in the project's spaces and wherever someone acts on the project's behalf.
